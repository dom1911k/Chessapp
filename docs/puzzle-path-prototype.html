    Scoring rules in this model

    BoardEasyMediumHardExpertStreak maxDailyPoints

    Every reward scales with the board factor (×1 Wood … ×2.75 Legend). Speed: 1 to 7 against a target time of 10 s + 8 s per extra move (×0.8 easy, ×1.3 hard, ×1.6 expert). Streak: +1 × factor per clean solve in a row after the first, up to +14 × factor. Fewer clues pay more: goal only +2, no clues +4 (× factor). Daily Solver (3rd clean solve) 60 × factor, Daily Ten 25 ×, perfect week 150 ×; streak moments at 15/30/50/100; every 100 solves +50 ×. Hard ×1.3 and Extra hard ×1.6 on base, streak and clue points. A miss costs nothing but resets the streak and eases the next puzzles a little; clean solves bring the difficulty back.
